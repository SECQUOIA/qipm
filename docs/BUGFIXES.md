# Confirmed bug fixes

Line references below describe the pre-fix files. “Empirical” means the defect was reproduced against the installed environment or a constructed fixture; “reading” means it follows directly from the implementation and the fixed paper specification.

## Critical result-integrity defects

- **F1 — Presolve output was discarded (`transform.py:227-245`).** Confirmed empirically and by reading: `getLp()` returned the original model after `presolve()`. This changed benchmark inputs and results. The transform now uses `getPresolvedLp()`, distinguishes definite and ambiguous unbounded outcomes, whitelists only reduced/not-reduced success statuses, records all outcomes, and rejects maximization or non-continuous models. Decompressed arrays are compared before replacement: unchanged output preserves downstream results, while changed output atomically purges results derived from the old `.std` before replacement; failed or withheld reruns also remove the stale file. JSON syntax, byte-level UTF-8 corruption, and non-object `.data` are replaced safely. Success-status publication is outside conversion error retraction, conversion exceptions record `error:<ExceptionName>` and are re-raised, and an empty MPS now records `error:RuntimeError` and retracts `.std` instead of warning and returning.
- **F4 — MNES fallback probed the null-space side (`benchmark.py:147-160, 211`).** Confirmed empirically with a wide matrix. Right probes could make the reported condition number exceed the true value, invalidating the lower-bound claim. MNES now probes unit left vectors through $\bar F^T$ for the smallest-singular-value fallback.
- **F7 — SPQR rank loss was accepted without verification (`benchmark.py:54-59`).** Confirmed empirically with a badly scaled independent row. This could inflate reported condition numbers beyond the true value. The row and column SPQR rank estimates must agree, and dropped rows are certified by bounded LSMR solves (`atol=btol=10^{-12}`) with a direct relative-residual gate of $10^{-6}$. Uncertified reductions receive `rank_uncertain`.

## High-impact correctness and reliability defects

- **F2 — Every structural zero row was dropped (`transform.py:255-259`).** Confirmed by reading and with a `0=b`, $b\ne0$ fixture. This could turn an infeasible LP into a feasible one. Only zero rows with $|b|\le10^{-9}$ are removed; other cases record `infeasible` and produce no `.std`.
- **F3 — Objective constants were lost (`transform.py:38-202`).** Confirmed by reading and objective-oracle tests. Variable shifts and `lp.offset_` changed the relationship between MPS and standard-form optima. The converter returns the accumulated constant and `.std` stores it as `obj_offset`.
- **F5 — Degenerate benchmarks wrote zero counts (`benchmark.py:215, 310-311`).** Confirmed empirically. Zero values poisoned logarithmic plots and result populations. Matrices with fewer than two rows or columns, including matrices reduced below two rows by SPQR, now record `skipped_degenerate` without count keys; the 100,000-row limit records `skipped_too_large`.
- **F6 — Empty non-basic partitions had wrong shortcuts and sparsity (`benchmark.py:181-185, 267-271`).** Confirmed by reading and objective-only-column reproduction. MNES now uses $s=1$ when $n_N=0$ and $\kappa=1$ when $A_N$ is empty or structurally zero. OSS with $n_N=0$ uses the row/column sparsity of $A$ and no nonexistent $z_\lambda$ term.
- **F8 — Arithmetic failures and both-mode coupling lost valid results (`benchmark.py:349-388`).** Confirmed empirically and by reading. Overflow, zero division, or an OSS error could erase MNES output; repetition truncation could undercount. Variants now have separate handlers, finite/overflow guards, condition numbers clamped to at least one, and exact ceiling repetition factors. Errors record `error:<ExceptionName>` with null value fields.
- **F9 — Singular-value and worker operations could hang or race (`benchmark.py:91-101, 201, 291`).** Confirmed by reading. `sparseqr` now imports at module load, both LM and SM `svds` calls use alarms, LM timeouts use valid maximum-norm probe lower bounds, probes have deadlines, and worker shutdown distinguishes timeout from crash after a final queue check.
- **F10 — Completion was inferred from poisoned value keys (`benchmark.py:445-473`, `solve.py:202-232`).** Confirmed by reading. Transform, benchmark, and solve now write explicit status keys. Benchmark validates integer-valued sparse metadata; load failures retract stale values and record `error:<ExceptionName>`. Transform clearing sanitizes corrupt/non-object ledgers before deleting each instance's `.std` and clears all downstream solve and benchmark fields. Benchmark mutation/clear/status readers, solve status reporting, and plot loading tolerate invalid JSON, invalid UTF-8, and non-object records. `--show` accepts only successful statuses, with a finite-number fallback for legacy JSON, and clear operations remove their corresponding statuses.
- **F10 addendum — Strict benchmark `.std` validation.** Benchmark loading now uses the same validated standard-form codec as solve. Corrupt CSR row pointers, out-of-range column indices, inconsistent dimensions, non-finite values, and sparse-index overflows now produce `error:<ExceptionName>` statuses instead of allowing SciPy to construct a matrix that could silently omit corrupt entries and yield an incorrect `ok` result. Benchmark also rejects archives with missing, wrong-length, or non-finite `c` or `b` members even though the benchmark calculation does not otherwise read them. This deliberate behavior change supersedes the partial benchmark-side validation described in F10.
- **F11 — Solve success, model construction, and retry timing were unchecked (`solve.py:20-82, 105-123`).** Confirmed empirically and by reading. Optimal model status is required; `.std` arrays, integer-valued structural metadata, and int32 ranges are validated before narrowing casts; HiGHS construction calls are checked; retry timing covers both attempts and records `ok_ipm`; worker crashes and timeouts are recorded; MPS integrality is rejected; stale docstrings were corrected.
- **F12 — Extraction clobbered JSON and trusted derived paths (`extract.py:93, 111, 150-155`).** Confirmed empirically and by reading. GLPK runtime data is merged atomically, with corrupt, invalid-UTF-8, and non-object existing ledgers treated as empty; derived destinations must remain under the cache root, extracted bytes use atomic replacement, and fresh clones record the source commit and UTC extraction time.
- **F14 — Result writes were non-atomic (`extract.py:95, 113, 152-155`; `transform.py:262-273`; `solve.py:100`; `benchmark.py:391`).** Confirmed by reading. Each affected script now writes through a unique temporary sibling, sets replacement files to mode `0644`, and uses `os.replace`; transform no longer uses a shared fixed `.npz` name.

## Medium and minor defects

- **F13 — One orphan or bad instance aborted a class (`transform.py:326-327`; `benchmark.py:429-430`).** Confirmed empirically with data-only directories. Transform, benchmark, and solve class loops isolate per-instance exceptions; transform and benchmark also warn and continue for missing inputs. Solve result merging recovers from corrupt existing JSON.
- **F15 — Plot filters and populations were inconsistent (`plot.py:99-205, 231-275`).** Confirmed empirically with zero, non-finite, single-valued, and unknown-class data. Runtime zero is retained; cycle counts must be positive and finite; crossover limits exclude non-finite values; both-mode curves use complete cases; histogram limits derive from data and widen equal bins; zero truncation keeps two points; curves and legends share one color map.
- **F16 — Documentation and utility code were stale (`README.md:34-173`; `requirements.txt:1-2`; `tests/visualise_npz.py:1-132`).** Confirmed by reading. The README now documents the OSS $(2n-1)/\varepsilon^2$ factor, statuses, size skip, objective offset, plotting’s LaTeX requirement, and correct dependencies. The requirements template header and dead `.sde`/`.init` visualizer branches were removed.

## Test gaps (F17)

- **Pipeline fixtures and regression coverage (`tests/test_transform.py`, `tests/test_benchmark.py`, `tests/test_solve.py`).** Confirmed empirically: the old fixtures presolved to empty or became benchmark-degenerate, while tests trusted golden NPZ files and skipped missing fixtures. Two MPS fixtures now empirically survive presolve with at least two rows and columns. Tests compare output dimensions to the actual presolved model, compare MPS and `.std` optima through `obj_offset`, directly guard zero-row and column-sign behavior, force the production wide-$\bar F$ left-probe fallback, and use a deterministic ill-conditioned duplicate to distinguish LSMR certification from the rejected LSQR tolerance. Controlled tests enforce the SPQR rank cross-check and all single/both post-reduction degeneracy paths; further coverage includes exact ceiling repetitions, excessive size, corrupt loads/data, stale-result retraction after successful and failed transforms, fractional structural metadata in solve and benchmark, merge preservation, and non-optimal MPS/std statuses. Benchmark tests skip before importing the module when `sparseqr` is unavailable; dead fixture-existence skips were removed.
